## 📖 About the Movie

**3 Idiots** is an *Indian Hindi-language comedy-drama* film directed by **Rajkumar Hirani**. It is loosely based on Chetan Bhagat's novel ***Five Point Someone***. The film follows three friends at an engineering college and challenges the pressure of rote learning and the fear of failure in education.

- Great friendship between the three main characters

  - Farhan, who wants to be a wildlife photographer
  - Raju, who struggles with fear and family pressure
